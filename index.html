Edge resilience

The internet goes down. The alarms still arrive.

Each customer site runs a GPU appliance that does the seeing. A cloud hub does the managing. They talk over an encrypted WireGuard tunnel using MQTT. Click a site to cut its link and watch it keep a write-ahead log, then replay every event the moment it reconnects.

GPU capacity

How many cameras fit on one GPU?

Frames from every camera are gathered into a 50 millisecond window and sent to the GPU as one batch. Pick a card from our field fleet and set how busy the scenes are. The planner sizes camera capacity from video memory, and the right side shows the batching live.

Forensic search

Search hours of footage by describing it.

Investigators do not want to scrub video. In MDI Veritas every detection is turned into a vector with a vision language model, stored in PostgreSQL with pgvector, and ranked by similarity to plain words. Try a query.

Every snapshot is a real YOLOv8 detection cut from the footage above. In the browser your words are matched against tags on each detection; in production MDI Veritas compares SigLIP 2 image and text embeddings (768 dimensions) with cosine similarity.

Secure delivery

A green build is not the same as shipping.

Appliances on customer premises never hold source code or repository keys. They receive a signed, edge-only bundle, and promotion to customers is a separate, deliberate step. Run a release, then try to slip a tampered one past a device.
